\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a Tic-\/\+Tac-\/\+Toe variant played on a pyramid-\/shaped board. 

The board consists of 3 rows\+: 1 cell on top, 3 in the middle, and 5 at the bottom. Winning requires 3 marks in a line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).
